SatsFlow is a non-custodial tipping and crowdfunding platform for Bitcoin and Monero. 99% of every donation lands on-chain in the creator's wallet. 1% funds the platform. No accounts held, no funds held, no middlemen.

How it works

  1. 1

    Creator signs up

    Pick a handle, add a BTC and/or XMR address. SatsFlow generates a public donation page.

  2. 2

    Donor picks an amount

    Enter a USD target or a specific amount of BTC/XMR. A fresh address is generated per donation.

  3. 3

    Payment splits on-chain

    When the donation is broadcast, the transaction has two outputs: 99% to the creator, 1% to the platform. Both are visible on-chain, forever.

  4. 4

    Creator sees it instantly

    Dashboard updates as confirmations arrive. Export history as CSV for taxes.
