Donations that go

SatsFlow is a non-custodial tipping and crowdfunding platform for Bitcoin and Monero. 99% of every donation lands on-chain in the creator's wallet. 1% funds the platform. No accounts held, no funds held, no middlemen.

How it works

  1. 1

    Creator adds an address

    Pick a handle, add a BTC and/or XMR address. SatsFlow generates a public donation page — no signup friction for donors.

  2. 2

    Donor picks an amount

    Enter a USD target or a specific amount of BTC/XMR. Add a display name and a message. A fresh address is generated per donation.

  3. 3

    Payment splits on-chain

    Two outputs: 99% to the creator, 1% to the platform. Both visible in any block explorer, forever. No intermediary wallet.

  4. 4

    Message appears instantly

    The donation and its message show up on the creator's dashboard and public feed as soon as the first confirmation lands.
